# Contact Information and Opening Hours

## Phone support
Nova Bank phone support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for card blocking, fraud reports, and urgent issues. General questions are answered by the AI assistant at any time. Human agents are available Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM, and Saturday, 9 AM to 2 PM, Eastern Time.

## Branches
Branches are open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday, 9 AM to 1 PM. Branches are closed on Sundays and on US federal holidays. Some services, such as PIN resets, updating identity documents, and opening home loans, require a branch visit with a valid photo ID.

## Other ways to reach us
Customers can send a secure message through the Nova Bank mobile app or online banking. Secure messages are answered within 1 business day. Nova Bank does not handle account requests over regular email or social media.

## Mailing address
Nova Bank, Customer Care, 1200 Harbor Avenue, Boston, MA 02110.

## Holidays
On federal holidays, branches are closed and transfers are not processed. Transfers scheduled on a holiday are processed on the next business day. Card payments and ATM withdrawals continue to work normally.
