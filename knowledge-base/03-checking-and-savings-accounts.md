# Checking and Savings Accounts

## Checking accounts
The Nova Everyday Checking account is used for daily spending, salary deposits, bill payments, and card purchases. It comes with a debit card. The monthly maintenance fee is 5 dollars. The fee is waived for any month in which the account balance stays above 1,000 dollars, or when the customer receives at least one direct deposit of 500 dollars or more.

## Savings accounts
The Nova Savings account earns interest, paid monthly near the end of each month. Interest rates depend on the savings plan: Basic Savings earns 0.5 percent per year, Plus Savings earns 1.0 percent, Goal Saver earns 2.5 percent, and Premium Savings earns 4.0 percent for balances above 10,000 dollars. Savings accounts have no monthly fee. Customers can make up to 6 withdrawals or transfers out of savings per month free of charge; each additional one costs 5 dollars.

## Automatic transfers to savings
Customers can set up a recurring transfer from checking to savings in the mobile app. Many customers schedule it right after payday.

## Account statuses
An account can be active, frozen, or dormant.
An active account works normally.
A frozen account has a temporary hold, most often because of a suspected fraud review. While frozen, card payments, transfers, and withdrawals are blocked, but incoming deposits are still accepted. Frozen accounts are usually reviewed within 2 business days. The customer may be asked to confirm recent transactions. A support ticket is created so the fraud team contacts the customer.
A dormant account has had no customer activity for over 12 months. To reactivate it, the customer must make a deposit or withdrawal, or visit a branch with photo ID. Dormant accounts are not charged the monthly fee.

## Balances
The current balance includes all posted transactions. Pending transactions, usually recent card purchases, are not final yet and normally post within 1 to 3 business days.
