# Disputing a Transaction

## When to dispute
Customers can dispute a card transaction if they were charged twice for the same purchase, charged the wrong amount, did not receive goods or services they paid for, or cancelled a subscription but were still charged. Unauthorized transactions the customer did not make are handled as fraud.

## Time limit
Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the transaction date.

## Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge is when the same merchant charges the same amount twice within a few minutes. The customer should identify which transaction is the duplicate. Many duplicate charges are pending authorizations that drop off on their own within 3 business days; if both charges have posted, a dispute should be opened.

## What happens after a dispute is opened
The transaction is marked as disputed. Nova Bank contacts the merchant. For disputes over 25 dollars, a temporary credit is usually added to the account within 10 business days while the case is investigated. Most disputes are resolved within 45 days. If the dispute is decided in the merchant's favor, the temporary credit is removed and the customer is notified.

## Before disputing
For simple problems, such as a wrong amount or an undelivered order, customers are encouraged to contact the merchant first, as this is often faster.
