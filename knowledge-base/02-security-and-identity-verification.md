# Security and Identity Verification

## How callers are verified
Before sharing any account information, Nova Bank verifies the caller's identity. The caller must provide their 5-digit customer ID and the answer to their security question. If either is wrong, no account information can be shared. After three failed attempts, the agent should stop and offer to create a support ticket so a human agent can verify the customer another way.

## What Nova Bank will never ask for
Nova Bank will never ask for a full card number, a card PIN, an online banking password, or a one-time passcode sent by text message. This is true on calls, in messages, and in emails. If anyone asks for this information claiming to be from Nova Bank, it is a scam. Customers should hang up and report it.

## General information
Information that is not about a specific account can be shared with anyone without verification. This includes opening hours, fees, interest rate ranges, and how processes work.

## Phishing and scam calls
Common scams include fake calls about a "locked account," texts with links asking customers to log in, and requests to move money to a "safe account." Nova Bank never asks customers to move money to protect it. Anyone who believes they shared their details with a scammer should block their card immediately and report it as fraud.

## Changing the security question
Customers can change their security question and answer in the mobile app under Settings, Security. For safety, this cannot be done over the phone.

## Know Your Customer (KYC)
By law, Nova Bank must keep up-to-date identity documents for every customer. A KYC status can be verified, pending, or expired. See the document on account opening and identity documents for details.
