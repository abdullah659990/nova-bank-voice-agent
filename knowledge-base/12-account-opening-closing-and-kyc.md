# Opening and Closing Accounts, and Identity Documents

## Opening an account
New customers can open a checking or savings account in the mobile app or at a branch. They need a valid government photo ID, a Social Security number, and a US address. Account opening usually takes 10 minutes; in some cases identity checks take up to 2 business days.

## Identity documents (KYC)
Nova Bank must keep valid identity documents for every customer. The KYC status can be verified, pending, or expired.
Verified means the documents are complete and valid.
Pending means documents were submitted and are being reviewed, which usually takes up to 2 business days.
Expired means the customer's ID document on file has expired. The customer must upload a new valid ID in the mobile app under Profile, Documents, or visit a branch. If documents stay expired for more than 60 days, some services, such as international transfers and new loans, may be restricted.

## Updating personal details
Phone number, email, and mailing address can be updated in the mobile app. Changing a legal name requires a branch visit with supporting documents.

## Closing an account
Customers can close an account at a branch or by sending a secure message. The balance must be zero or positive, there must be no pending transactions, and any linked loans or recurring payments must be moved to another account first. The remaining balance is sent by check or transferred to another bank.
