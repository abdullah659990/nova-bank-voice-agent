# Debit Cards

## Daily limits
Each debit card has a daily spending limit of 500, 1,000, 2,000, or 5,000 dollars. The daily ATM withdrawal limit is 800 dollars for all cards. Customers can request a limit change; the AI assistant should create a support ticket for this.

## Card statuses
A card can be active, blocked, lost, or expired.
Active cards work normally.
Blocked cards cannot be used for any payment or withdrawal. Cards are blocked when the customer reports suspected fraud, or by the bank's fraud team.
Lost cards have been reported lost or stolen by the customer. They are permanently disabled and must be replaced.
Expired cards have passed their expiry date and no longer work.

## Lost or stolen cards
If a card is lost or stolen, it should be blocked immediately. Once blocked, it cannot be unblocked; a replacement card must be ordered. The customer is not responsible for unauthorized transactions made after the card was reported.

## Replacement cards
Standard replacement cards are free and arrive in 5 to 7 business days. Express delivery costs 25 dollars and arrives in 2 business days. The new card has a new card number. Recurring payments, such as subscriptions, must be updated with the new card details by the customer.

## Expired cards
Nova Bank automatically sends a new card about 30 days before the old card expires. If the card has already expired and the customer did not receive a new one, the agent can order a replacement for free.

## Declined cards
A card may be declined because the card is blocked, lost, or expired; the account is frozen; there is not enough money in the account; the daily limit has been reached; or the purchase looked unusual and was stopped by fraud protection. Checking the card status and the account status usually explains the reason.

## PIN
A forgotten PIN can only be reset at a branch with photo ID or at a Nova Bank ATM using the app. Nova Bank staff and the AI assistant will never ask for a PIN.
