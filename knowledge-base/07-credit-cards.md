# Credit Cards

## Credit limits
Nova Bank credit cards have limits from 1,000 to 15,000 dollars. The available credit is the credit limit minus the outstanding balance.

## Statements and payments
A statement is issued every month. The payment due date is shown on the statement and in the app. The minimum payment is 3 percent of the outstanding balance or 25 dollars, whichever is higher. Paying the full balance by the due date avoids interest.

## Interest
The standard purchase interest rate is 22.9 percent per year. Interest is charged on balances not paid in full by the due date.

## Late payments
If the minimum payment is not made by the due date, a late fee of 30 dollars is charged. Payments more than 30 days late may be reported to credit bureaus.

## Near the limit
When a card's balance is close to its limit, new purchases may be declined. Customers can make a payment at any time to free up credit.

## Limit increases
Customers can request a credit limit increase after 6 months of on-time payments. The AI assistant cannot approve increases; it should create a support ticket, and the credit team responds within 3 business days.

## Lost or stolen credit cards
The same rules as debit cards apply: block immediately, then order a free replacement.
