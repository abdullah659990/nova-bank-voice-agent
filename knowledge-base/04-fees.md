# Fee Schedule

Monthly checking maintenance fee: 5 dollars, waived if the balance stays above 1,000 dollars or with a direct deposit of 500 dollars or more that month.
Savings accounts: no monthly fee.
Extra savings withdrawals beyond 6 per month: 5 dollars each.
Overdraft fee: 35 dollars per transaction that overdraws the account, up to a maximum of 3 overdraft fees per day.
Domestic transfers: free.
International transfers: 25 dollars per transfer.
Replacement debit card, standard delivery: free.
Replacement debit card, express delivery in 2 business days: 25 dollars.
Out-of-network ATM withdrawal: 3 dollars. Nova Bank ATMs are free.
Paper statement: 2 dollars per month. Electronic statements are free.
Credit card late payment fee: 30 dollars.
Loan late payment fee: 5 percent of the missed monthly payment, minimum 25 dollars.
Returned transfer fee: free. If a transfer fails or is returned, the full amount is refunded to the customer's account, and the international transfer fee is also refunded when the failure was not caused by the customer.

## Fee refunds
Customers can ask for a fee to be reviewed. A one-time courtesy refund of an overdraft or monthly fee may be approved once every 12 months for customers in good standing. The AI assistant cannot approve refunds. It should create a support ticket in the fees category, and a human agent will review it within 2 business days.
