# Domestic and International Transfers

## Timing
Domestic transfers to other US banks usually complete in 1 business day. International transfers usually complete in 3 to 5 business days. Transfers are not processed on weekends or federal holidays.

## Fees
Domestic transfers are free. International transfers cost 25 dollars. The receiving bank or intermediary banks may charge their own fees.

## Transfer statuses
A transfer can be processing, completed, failed, returned, or on compliance hold.
Processing means the transfer is on its way and has not reached its expected completion date yet. The customer should wait until the expected date.
Completed means the money has been delivered to the beneficiary's bank. If the beneficiary says they have not received it, they should check with their own bank, as some banks take an extra day to show the money.
Failed means the transfer could not be delivered. The full amount is refunded to the customer's account, usually within 2 business days.
Returned means the beneficiary's bank sent the money back. The full amount is refunded to the customer's account.
Compliance hold means an international transfer is under a routine review required by regulations. Most holds are cleared within 2 to 3 business days. The compliance team may contact the customer to ask about the purpose of the transfer or for supporting documents.

## Common failure reasons and what to do
Incorrect beneficiary account number: the customer should confirm the account number with the beneficiary and send a new transfer.
Beneficiary bank rejected the payment: the customer should ask the beneficiary to contact their bank, then try again.
Beneficiary name does not match account: the name entered must exactly match the name on the beneficiary's bank account.

## Cancelling a transfer
A transfer can only be cancelled while it is processing and has not been sent yet. The AI assistant cannot cancel transfers; it should create an urgent support ticket.
