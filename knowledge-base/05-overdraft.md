# Overdraft

## What an overdraft is
An overdraft happens when a payment is made for more money than is available in the checking account. If the customer has overdraft coverage, Nova Bank pays the transaction and the balance becomes negative.

## Overdraft limits
Overdraft limits are 0, 250, or 500 dollars, depending on the customer's account history. A limit of 0 means overdraft coverage is not active, and payments that would overdraw the account are declined instead.

## Fees
Each transaction that overdraws the account costs 35 dollars, with a maximum of 3 overdraft fees per day. No fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by 5 dollars or less.

## Bringing the account back
A negative balance should be brought back above zero within 30 days. Any deposit, including salary, automatically covers the negative balance first. If the account stays negative for more than 30 days, overdraft coverage may be removed.

## Changing overdraft settings
Customers can turn overdraft coverage off in the mobile app. Requests to increase the overdraft limit are reviewed by a human agent; the AI assistant should create a support ticket for these requests.
