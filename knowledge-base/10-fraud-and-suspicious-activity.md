# Fraud and Suspicious Activity

## Signs of fraud
Signs include transactions the customer does not recognize, purchases in foreign countries the customer has not visited, several large online purchases in a short time, cryptocurrency purchases the customer did not make, and alerts about login attempts from unknown devices.

## What to do immediately
If a customer reports transactions they did not make, the card involved must be blocked right away to stop further charges. Then an urgent fraud ticket must be created listing the transactions the customer does not recognize. The fraud team contacts the customer within 4 hours.

## Zero liability
Customers are not responsible for unauthorized card transactions reported promptly. Confirmed fraudulent charges are refunded in full, usually within 10 business days after the investigation.

## Frozen accounts
If the fraud team detects suspicious activity, the account may be frozen as a precaution. See the checking and savings accounts document for what a frozen account means.

## Replacement after fraud
After a card is blocked for fraud, a replacement card should be ordered. Express delivery is free when the replacement is needed because of fraud.

## Protecting yourself
Never share a PIN, password, or one-time passcode. Turn on transaction alerts in the mobile app. Check transactions regularly.
