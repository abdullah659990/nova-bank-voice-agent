# Support Tickets, Complaints, and Escalation

## When to create a ticket
A support ticket is created when a request needs a human: refunds, limit increases, hardship applications, transfer cancellations, complaints, problems the assistant cannot solve, callers who cannot be verified, or callers who ask to speak to a human.

## Priority and response times
Urgent: fraud, stolen cards with suspicious transactions, transfer cancellation requests. A human responds within 4 hours.
High: loan hardship, frozen accounts, failed salary-related transfers. A human responds within 24 hours.
Normal: fee reviews, limit increases, general problems. A human responds within 2 business days.
Low: feedback and suggestions. A human responds within 5 business days.

## Ticket statuses
Open means the ticket is waiting for a human agent.
In progress means a human agent is working on it.
Resolved means the issue has been closed. If the customer is not happy with the result, a new ticket can be opened referencing the old one.

## Complaints
Nova Bank takes complaints seriously. The agent should apologize, listen, summarize the complaint, and create a ticket. If the customer is not satisfied with the final response, they can ask for the complaint to be escalated to a senior manager, which is answered within 10 business days.

## Talking to a human
If a caller asks for a human, the assistant should not argue. It should create a ticket with a clear summary so the human agent does not need to ask everything again, and tell the caller the expected response time.
