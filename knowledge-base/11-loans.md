# Loans

## Loan types
Personal loans: 5,000 to 40,000 dollars, terms of 24 to 60 months, interest rates from 8 to 18 percent per year.
Auto loans: 10,000 to 50,000 dollars, terms of 36 to 72 months, interest rates from 4 to 9 percent per year.
Home loans (mortgages): 150,000 to 600,000 dollars, terms of 15, 20, or 30 years, interest rates from 3 to 7 percent per year. Home loan applications require a branch visit.

## Monthly payments
Loan payments are collected automatically from the customer's checking account on the due date. Customers should make sure enough money is in the account.

## Loan statuses
A loan can be current, late, or paid off.
Current means all payments are up to date.
Late means a payment is overdue. The number of days past due is shown on the loan.
Paid off means the loan has been fully repaid.

## Late payments
There is a 15-day grace period after the due date. After that, a late fee of 5 percent of the missed payment, with a minimum of 25 dollars, is charged. Payments more than 30 days late are reported to credit bureaus, which can lower the customer's credit score. Customers who are late should pay the overdue amount as soon as possible, through the app or at a branch.

## Financial hardship
Customers who cannot make payments because of job loss, illness, or other hardship can apply for the hardship program, which may temporarily lower or pause payments. The AI assistant should create a high-priority support ticket so a loan specialist calls the customer.

## Early payoff
Personal and auto loans can be paid off early with no penalty. Home loans can be paid off early; a fee of 1 percent may apply in the first 3 years. To get an exact payoff amount, the customer should request it through a support ticket or the app.

## New loan applications
Customers can apply for personal and auto loans in the mobile app. The AI assistant can explain loan types and rates but cannot approve loans.
